## 4.3 Food Descriptions, Allergens, and Food Safety

Soon (2018) identifies gaps in Malaysian packaged-food allergen labelling; Tan et al. (2023) find limited allergy knowledge among Malaysian restaurant staff. These suggest different weaknesses—written information and staff communication—without establishing ReMeal’s practices. Malaysia’s Food Regulations 1985 include allergen-labelling requirements, whose application to restaurant app listings needs separate assessment.

Koidis et al.’s (2024) UK online-ordering study adds evidence of unclear information and cross-contact concerns. One of eleven tested meals exceeded the gluten-free threshold. Its small, self-selected sample cannot establish prevalence on ReMeal, but severe allergy risks make information gaps consequential (Valenta et al., 2015).

For descriptions, Hou et al. (2017) found pictures paired with common descriptive food names improved attitudes and purchase intentions. Unlike the safety research, this concerns choice and presentation; it supports investigating ambiguous images without proving that ReMeal’s photos misrepresent food.

## 5.3 Food Descriptions and Photos

We reviewed four listings from Dorsett Hartamas Kuala Lumpur and Krem Tarik, Kota Damansara. The screenshots were captured during 1–3 November 2026 (Figs. A21–A24).

**Table 1. Observed listing details**

| Listing | Information shown | Missing or unclear |
|---|---|---|
| Dorsett breakfast, RM27 (was RM45) | Dine-in buffet; children’s menu; 50 left; 06:30–10:00 | Dishes, ingredients, allergens; description instead ends at 10:30 |
| Dorsett Saturday hi-tea, RM79 (was RM158) | Dine-in; same-week Saturday redemption; 13:00–17:00; 50 left | Dishes, ingredients, allergens |
| Krem mystery scoop, RM6 (was RM11) | Four possible flavours; “Contains dairy”; 3 left | Actual flavour, portion size, other allergens |
| Krem waffle and scoop, RM8 (was RM12) | Waffle with mystery scoop; “Contains dairy”; 1 left | Waffle ingredients, actual flavour, other allergens; drink inclusion |

Krem’s named flavour range limits uncertainty, whereas Dorsett names no dishes. The waffle photo includes an unexplained drink. These ambiguities weaken informed choice, but we did not buy the food and cannot establish photographic inaccuracy.

All four listings show discounts and remaining stock; store backgrounds display CO₂e estimates. We did not verify the reference prices, counts or calculation methods. Scarcity may create urgency, but manipulation is unproven. Clear collection conditions are a strength. Clauses 1.06 and 3.01 support truthful descriptions and visible trade-offs; improvements include consistent times, portion sizes and clarification of photo contents.

## 5.4 Allergen and Food Safety Information

The Terms disclaim a duty to provide ingredients or allergens and direct customers to vendors (§ 5.3; Fig. A10). They also disclaim completeness of vendor information (ReMeal, 2024b, § 9.3; Fig. A14).

Neither Dorsett listing states allergens. Krem states dairy, but its waffle ingredients and other allergens remain unspecified (Figs. A21–A24). Outside this sample, Sunset Terrace lists possible allergen traces (Fig. A17). Absence of a warning does not establish an allergen’s presence. Asking vendors still depends on staff knowledge and safe handling, including prevention of cross-contact (Tan et al., 2023).

Clause 1.03 connects safe software to allergen-information fields and warnings; 1.04 supports reporting reasonably identified dangers associated with software or documentation. These duties justify investigating disclosure gaps, not asserting an injury occurred. Four listings cannot represent the platform; other screens and actual food handling were not assessed.
